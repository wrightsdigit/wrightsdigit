# Wrightsdigit website

Static HTML/CSS/JS website for Wrightsdigit.

## Files
- index.html — main website and three separate order forms
- services.html — service details
- privacy.html — starter privacy policy
- terms.html — starter terms page
- style.css — responsive styling
- script.js — navigation, order tabs, validation and FormSubmit submission

## Order email
The forms are configured for `wrightsdigit@gmail.com` through FormSubmit.

## Large files
Direct uploads are checked client-side at 10 MB total. For larger artwork packages, customers should use Google Drive, Dropbox or OneDrive and paste the shareable link into the form.

## Before launch
1. Submit a test order and complete any FormSubmit activation/confirmation email.
2. Replace placeholder portfolio artwork with your own work.
3. Confirm your actual pricing, revision, refund and turnaround policies.
4. Review the starter legal pages for your jurisdiction.
5. Test the cloud links and email delivery on desktop and mobile.
