Privacy Policy

This starter policy explains how information submitted through the Wrightsdigit website is intended to be handled. Review and adapt it to your final business and legal requirements before launch.

Information you submit

When you place an order, we may receive your name, order name, email address, phone number, company information, project specifications, messages, uploaded files and cloud-storage links.

How we use it

We use submitted information to review artwork, communicate about an order, prepare requested work, provide quotes and deliver files or other services you request.

Third-party services

Order forms may use FormSubmit for email delivery. Customers may also provide links hosted by Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive or another file-storage provider. Those services have their own privacy policies and access controls.

File access

Only provide artwork you are authorized to share. For cloud links, make sure the link allows the Wrightsdigit team to access the required files without requesting unnecessary account permissions.

Contact

For privacy questions, email wrightsdigit@gmail.com.
