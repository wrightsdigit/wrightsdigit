06 / FAQ

Good questions.
Clear answers.

What files can I send? +

AI, EPS, PDF, PNG and JPG are good starting formats. Existing embroidery files can also be supplied when relevant. For files over 10 MB, use a Google Drive, Dropbox or OneDrive link.

How fast can I receive my file? +

Turnaround depends on complexity and current workload. Your order review can confirm the expected delivery time.

Can you handle complex logos? +

Yes. Send the artwork and describe the intended garment or product. We can assess the design before quoting.

Do you provide revisions? +

Revision terms can be set in your final Wrightsdigit policy. The site is structured so this can be communicated clearly to customers.
